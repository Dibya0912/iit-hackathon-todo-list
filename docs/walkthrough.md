# LevelForge walkthrough — approximately 150 seconds

Use a dedicated demo account with real persisted quests. Never insert balances into production signup.
Record at 1280×720 or 1920×1080, hide unrelated tabs and terminals, disable personal notifications.

- **0–20 seconds:** Show the introduction. “LevelForge turns everyday intentions into a personal adventure. Create a quest, do the activity, and mark it complete to grow your character.” Sign up with a disposable demo email or sign into your prepared demo account.
- **20–45 seconds:** Open New quest. Add a concrete title, category, and difficulty. Explain the server-confirmed XP and Gold preview.
- **45–70 seconds:** Complete the quest. Show the reward notification, new Gold balance, attribute XP, and streak. “Completion is self-reported; the app protects the integrity of the saved rewards.”
- **70–95 seconds:** Complete a second Hard quest to reach 100 XP and level 2. Show 0/200 toward level 3. For a prepared account, ensure the next real quest crosses a threshold.
- **95–110 seconds:** Refresh the browser. Show that the character, session, and completion history remain. Briefly show the completed tab in the journal.
- **110–135 seconds:** Purchase First Light for 30 Gold. Open Inventory and equip it. Return to the dashboard to show the sun badge.
- **135–150 seconds:** Show the profile and activity ledger. “Different quests grow different attributes. Small, honest steps become progress you can see.”

## Recording and publishing checklist

1. Rehearse with the actual application and MySQL running.
2. Use a fresh demo account for the final take; normal signup always starts with zero progress.
3. Record with OBS or the operating system recorder at 720p, 30 fps, H.264, around 3 Mbps.
4. A 150-second recording at 3 Mbps is roughly 57 MB plus audio. Confirm the actual final file is under 100 MB.
5. Play the entire recording and check readable text, audible narration, and absence of private data.
6. Upload to a service you control with public or unlisted access that does not require login.
7. Open the share URL in a signed-out browser to verify access.
8. Add only that verified URL to the README/submission.
